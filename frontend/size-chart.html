Sizing Guide

Men's Racing Suits

Our suits are sized to European standards. Take your chest, waist, and hip measurements and compare against the chart below. The suit is designed to be worn in a riding position, so a slightly relaxed fit when standing is normal.

Pro Tip — Suits

The most important measurement is your chest. If you're between sizes, choose the larger size for comfort in the riding position. All measurements are in centimetres (cm).

Sizing Guide

Racing Gloves

Measure the circumference of your hand at the widest point (excluding the thumb). Use the chart below to find your international size for both men's and women's gloves.

Pro Tip — Gloves

Wrap a tape measure around your dominant hand at the knuckles, keeping your fingers together and thumb out. This is your hand circumference — match it to the chart below. Gloves should feel snug but allow full finger movement.

Sizing Guide

Motorbike Racing Boots

Our boots follow European sizing. Measure your foot from the tip of the longest toe to the back of your heel. Compare your foot length (in cm) to the chart below to find your size across all major international standards.

Pro Tip — Boots

Measure both feet and use the larger measurement. Feet are typically slightly larger in the afternoon — measure then for the best fit. Riding boots should feel firm but comfortable with a thin riding sock.
