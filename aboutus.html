The Origin of JMX

JMX Motorsports was born not in a boardroom, but on the road — from a frustration shared by every serious rider who ever struggled to find gear that was both technically sound and genuinely built for performance. Our founders, veteran riders themselves, saw a gap between what was available and what riders actually needed.

"We didn't want to make cheaper versions of someone else's gear. We wanted to make the best gear in the world — and prove it could be done from Pakistan."

Starting with a small manufacturing workshop in Sialkot — a city long recognised as a global hub for sporting goods excellence — JMX began crafting motorcycle suits by hand, obsessing over every detail: seam strength, armour placement, abrasion resistance, and the often-overlooked factor of long-distance comfort.

Sialkot Workshop, Est. 2015
Our Journey

Milestones That Shaped JMX

2015
Founded in Sialkot

JMX opens its first workshop, producing custom motorcycle suits for local riders and regional distributors.

2017
First Export Order

JMX gear crosses borders for the first time, shipping to clients in the UK and Germany. International quality standards are adopted across all product lines.

2019
CE Level 2 Certification

The entire armour range achieves CE Level 2 certification — the highest standard in rider protection, putting JMX on par with European market leaders.

2022
Full Product Range Launch

Expansion beyond suits into gloves, boots, hoodies, balaclavas, body protectors and accessories — a complete head-to-toe ecosystem for every rider.

2026
20+ Countries & Counting

JMX Motorsports now reaches riders across Europe, the Middle East, North America and beyond — with the new 2026 collection representing our most technically advanced range to date.

Manufacturer & Exporter · Est. 2015

Sialkot, Pakistan has been supplying the world with world-class sporting goods for over 125 years — from footballs used in FIFA World Cups to surgical instruments trusted in operating theatres globally. JMX carries forward that legacy of precision manufacturing and international-grade quality.

When you wear JMX, you're wearing the output of a city that has made excellence in manufacturing its identity. Our craftspeople don't just assemble garments — they build armour for riders who rely on it when it matters most.

We are not just a Pakistani brand that exports. We are proof that the best motorcycle gear in the world can come from Pakistan — and increasingly, does. Every order placed with JMX supports skilled employment, ethical manufacturing, and the continued growth of Pakistan's world-class sporting goods sector.
